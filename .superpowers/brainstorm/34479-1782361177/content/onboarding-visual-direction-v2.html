Onboarding Visual Direction

Choose the asset tone for the full-app setup gate. The final page will use Atropos tilt, a Letter Glitch background, and compact manual setup steps.

A. Neon Terminal

Dark graphite shell, cyan/violet rim light. Best match for a blocked coding monitor: technical, native to the dark app, and least likely to read as a marketing welcome page.

B. Dark Editorial

Warmer beige hardware against a dark scene. More human and tactile, but it pulls the setup screen away from the settled near-black monitor language.

C. Lamplit Ivory

Friendliest and most physical. Good for a first-run setup moment, but high risk of feeling like a separate onboarding brand rather than OhMyC's monitor failure state.
