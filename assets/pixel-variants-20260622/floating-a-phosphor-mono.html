// floating sidebar · phosphor mono

What changed: sidebar has no background color, no right border, no section dividers, no keycap boxes. Nav items are just text floating on the page background. Active item gets a phosphor bullet to its left + brighter text. Hover nudges the item 2px to the right (no background fill).

Animation: on page load the whole sidebar slides in from translateX(-40px), then each child cascades in 40ms apart with translateX(-14px) → 0. Click ↻ replay slide-in top-right to see it again.

Trade-off: works because the page bg is solid dark. If the content area had a busy background (image / texture), the floating nav would need text-shadow or a subtle backdrop-blur pill behind active items to stay readable.
