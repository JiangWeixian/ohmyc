// floating sidebar + collapse · phosphor mono

Two states:
• expanded (220px) — full labels, brand, library sub-nav, search field, status text
• collapsed (56px) — just the key caps M T A S C P, plus a tiny ⌘K and a single pulsing live-dot. Brand becomes a ◊ glyph.

Toggle: chevron button parked at the sidebar's right edge (rides along with the width transition), or hit [ / ] on the keyboard. Esc also expands.

Tooltips: in collapsed mode, hovering a key cap floats the full name out to the right in a small phosphor-bordered card, so you never lose navigability.

Active state: in expanded mode it's a phosphor bullet to the left; in collapsed mode the active key cap itself glows phosphor with a double-layer shadow. Same signal, scaled down.

Animation: 320ms cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1) on width, chevron rotates 180°, labels fade on a faster 200ms opacity. Sub-nav and status text fade out; the live dot stays pulsing throughout.
