   **Why only these 4:** Phase 14 needs animated dialog, tabs, tooltip, and button for sidebar and card migration. Phase 15 will add more as needed. The `animated-sidebar`, `animated-select`, `animated-list`, `animated-navbar`, `command-palette`, and `toast-notification` are either not reusable (demo quality) or not needed until specific Phase 14/15 plans. They can be copied in those phases.
